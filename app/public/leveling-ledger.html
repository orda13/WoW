    The Leveling Ledger add-on records exact XP for each play session, training costs, vendor and Merchant's Favor prices, and what drops for you. When you log out or type /reload, the game saves its file and this PC imports it within a few seconds.

    Checking which file is being watched…

      Installing the add-on
      1. Copy the addon\LevelingLedger folder from the project into your Forever game folder's Interface\AddOns, so you have Interface\AddOns\LevelingLedger\LevelingLedger.toc.
      2. At character select, open AddOns and make sure Leveling Ledger is ticked. If it shows as out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.
      3. Play as usual. Talk to your class trainer and open vendors once so it can read their prices. Type /ledger in game to see what it has recorded.

      Session history

      New in World of Warcraft: Forever

      Forever adds new instances and areas. Their bosses and loot aren't published in Blizzard's data or QuestieDB yet, so this shows their quests. Use the Wowhead link for anything else.

      Boss lists are from the original game Forever is based on; Forever may add or change bosses. Creature levels, ranks and the loot shown come from QuestieDB's Forever data, checked against Blizzard's Forever item data: only items that still exist in Forever are listed, so loot is partial. Forever replaced most classic dungeon loot with new items that aren't published yet. Items with a drop chance come from Wowhead's Forever tooltips, looked up for items already known from QuestieDB. Each boss links to its Wowhead Forever page for the full loot table.

      Abilities by level
