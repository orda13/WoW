        Installing the add-on
        1. Copy the addon\LevelingLedger folder from the project into your Forever game folder's Interface\AddOns, so you have Interface\AddOns\LevelingLedger\LevelingLedger.toc.
        2. At character select, open AddOns and make sure Leveling Ledger is ticked. If it shows as out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.
        3. Play as usual. Talk to your class trainer and open vendors once so it can read their prices, and open each profession window once for your recipe lists. Type /ledger in game to see what it has recorded.
        4. Type /ledger alts for your characters' details, bags and bank. Open each character's bank once so it's recorded. Item tooltips show which of your other characters hold the item.
        5. A small panel in game shows session time, XP per hour, time to level, kills, gold earned and rested XP. Drag it where you like; /ledger lock, /ledger hide, /ledger show and /ledger reset control it.

          New in World of Warcraft: Forever

          Forever adds new instances and areas. Their bosses and loot aren't published in Blizzard's data or QuestieDB yet, so this shows their quests. Use the Wowhead link for anything else.

          Boss lists are from the original game Forever is based on; Forever may add or change bosses. Creature levels, ranks and the loot shown come from QuestieDB's Forever data, checked against Blizzard's Forever item data: only items that still exist in Forever are listed, so loot is partial. Forever replaced most classic dungeon loot with new items; for the dungeons pasted from Wowhead's Forever dungeon journal, its Drops list is added too, and MSPaintWoW's guides add pull order, drop chances, best-in-slot marks and quest rewards for the dungeons they cover. AtlasLoot Continued's Forever loot tables fill in items none of these list, and the maps show standard routes from the Forever Dungeon Routes add-on (each boss's wing shown for Scarlet Monastery, Stratholme, Dire Maul and Blackrock Spire). Items with a drop chance come from Wowhead's Forever tooltips, looked up for items already known from QuestieDB. Each boss links to its Wowhead Forever page for the full loot table.

          Abilities by level

          Spells and ranks your class learns at each level, from Forever's game files. Cast time, range and cooldown come from Wowhead's Forever tooltips. Training costs come from the add-on when you open your class trainer (shown locked, "From the trainer"); you can type any it hasn't seen. The budget adds them up against the gold you carry.
